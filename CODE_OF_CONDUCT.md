# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Contributors should be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy, and
avoid discriminatory, threatening, or disruptive conduct.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior threatens the safety or health of the community. Report conduct
concerns through the repository's [private report form](../../security/advisories/new)
and put `Code of conduct report` in the title. If private reporting is not
enabled, open a public issue asking for a private contact without naming people
or describing the incident. Reports will be handled with discretion and
without retaliation.
